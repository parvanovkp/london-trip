Tue 13.10

Arrival

Travel

Wed 14.10

Museum to Westminster on foot, dinner in Pimlico

SoloDinner: Sashi & Desi
  • 09:30Tower Hill → South Kensington, District or Circle line direct (~25 min).
  • 10:00Natural History Museum: free, opens 10:00. Hintze Hall and the dinosaurs first; two hours covers the highlights.
  • 12:15Royal Albert Hall from outside and the Albert Memorial opposite. Lunch around South Kensington.
  • 13:00Kensington Gardens → Hyde Park past the Serpentine to Hyde Park Corner (~35 min walk).
  • 14:15Buckingham Palace via Constitution Hill, then St James's Park; the bridge over the lake has the classic palace view.
  • 15:15Big Ben, Parliament and Westminster Abbey; the London Eye is across Westminster Bridge.
  • 16:15National Gallery on Trafalgar Square, free, open to 18:00.
  • 17:45Leicester Square → Chinatown, a few minutes apart.
  • 19:00Dinner with Sashi and Desi at the Marquis of Westminster, 50 Warwick Way: District or Circle from Embankment to Victoria (3 stops), then 8 min on foot. Gastropub, British and modern European.
Changing of the Guard? Usually Mon, Wed and Fri at 11:00. To catch it, start at the palace by 10:30 and walk west to the museum instead; check the Household Division calendar the day before.
Thu 15.10

Camden, the canal and Notting Hill

SoloDinner: Desi & Antoine
  • 08:00Optional early start: Old Spitalfields Thursday antiques market, 15 min from the hostel.
  • 10:00The Garden at 120, 120 Fenchurch St: free rooftop garden from Laura's list, no booking, airport-style security. 10 min walk from Wombat's; half an hour is plenty.
  • 10:45Head to Camden Town, ~30 min: Circle or Metropolitan from Aldgate to King's Cross, then Northern line.
  • 11:15Camden Market: Camden Lock, the Stables Market and Cyberdog. Lunch from the food stalls.
  • 13:30A drink at The Dark Horse: pub downstairs, open-air rooftop terrace above.
  • 14:30Regent's Canal towpath from Camden Lock to Little Venice, ~4 km past the zoo and Regent's Park. Primrose Hill detour for the skyline: +20 min.
  • 16:00Little Venice → Portobello Road, ~25 min walk.
  • 16:30Notting Hill's pastel streets: Lancaster Road, St Luke's Mews, Elgin Crescent. The street market closes at 13:00 on Thursdays; shops and cafés stay open. Sunset ~18:10.
  • 19:00Dinner with Desi and Antoine (pick below). Home on the Hammersmith & City line from Ladbroke Grove straight to Aldgate East.

Fri 16.10

Sat 17.10

White Cliffs of Dover and Canterbury

With Sashi & Laura

Not final yet: Sashi and Laura will decide closer to the day.

  • 09:00Leave Bromley for Dover by car (~1 h 30 min).
  • 10:30White Cliffs visitor centre (National Trust, CT16 1HJ). Parking £6, free for members; gates lock at 19:00.
  • 10:45Cliff-top walk to South Foreland Lighthouse, ~55 min each way; France on a clear day. Lighthouse and Mrs Knott's Tearoom open 11:00–17:00 on Saturdays this month. Turn back earlier for a shorter walk.
  • 12:45Lunch on the beach at The Coastguard, St Margaret's Bay, 10 min from the car park. Sashi can book once the day is settled, or eat in Canterbury instead.
  • 14:15Drive to Canterbury (~35 min).
  • 15:00Canterbury Cathedral: Saturdays 10:00–17:00, last entry 16:00, £18. Booking online gets fast-track entry. Choral Evensong at 17:30.
  • 16:30Old town: Christ Church Gate, Mercery Lane, the River Stour and the Westgate Towers. Early dinner there.
  • 19:00Drive back to Bromley (~1 h 15 min).

If it rains heavily: Canterbury only

  • 10:00Leave Bromley (~1 h 15 min).
  • 11:15Canterbury Cathedral; allow about two hours inside.
  • 13:30Lunch in an old-town pub, then the lanes and shops around Mercery Lane.
  • 17:30Choral Evensong in the cathedral, then dinner and the drive home.
Bring: proper shoes and a windproof layer for the cliffs; the path is exposed and muddy after rain.
